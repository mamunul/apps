SHAREDCOST

PRIVACY POLICY

Date of Last Update: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SharedCost ("SharedCost", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the SharedCost application and related services.

By using SharedCost, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. Where consent is required by applicable law, we will obtain it separately.

1. Information We Collect

SharedCost may collect information that you provide directly when you create an account, create or manage projects, add participants, record expenses or deposits, or contact us.

Account Information

If you create an account, we may collect information necessary to authenticate and maintain your account, including your email address and authentication information provided through your chosen authentication method.

Project and Financial Information

Depending on how you use SharedCost, information stored in the application may include:

  • Project names and project-related information.
  • Participant names and identifiers.
  • Participant email addresses when used for sharing or invitations.
  • Expenses, costs, amounts, descriptions, dates, and related information.
  • Deposits, amounts, dates, and related information.
  • Project sharing and participation information.
  • Version history and changes made to project data.

This information is used to provide the core functionality of SharedCost, including synchronizing projects across supported devices and allowing you to share projects with other participants.

Support Information

If you contact us for support, feedback, or to report a problem, we may collect your email address, name if provided, and the contents of your message.

2. Information Collected Automatically

When you use SharedCost, certain technical, diagnostic, and usage information may be collected automatically by the application and its service providers.

Depending on the services enabled, this information may include:

  • Device type and operating system version.
  • Application version and language.
  • Country or region information.
  • Application launch and session information.
  • Interactions with application features.
  • Diagnostic and crash information.
  • Technical identifiers used by service providers to support analytics and application functionality.

We use this information to maintain application security, diagnose technical problems, understand application performance, measure application usage, and improve the reliability and functionality of SharedCost.

3. Firebase and Google Analytics

SharedCost uses Google Firebase services to support authentication, cloud data storage, synchronization, notifications, application security, diagnostics, and other application functionality.

Where Firebase Analytics is enabled, SharedCost uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how users interact with the application.

Analytics information may include:

  • Application launches and session activity.
  • Application version and device information.
  • Interactions with application features.
  • Automatically collected usage and diagnostic events.
  • Technical identifiers used by Firebase Analytics.

We may use analytics information to:

  • Understand application usage and feature engagement.
  • Improve application functionality and user experience.
  • Identify technical problems.
  • Measure the effectiveness of application advertising campaigns and app installation campaigns.

We do not intentionally send passwords, payment card information, expense descriptions, or other sensitive project contents to Firebase Analytics.

Google processes information according to its applicable privacy policies and service terms.

  • Firebase Privacy Information

4. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information for the following purposes:

  • To create and authenticate user accounts.
  • To provide and maintain SharedCost functionality.
  • To store and synchronize project data.
  • To allow users to share projects with participants.
  • To send notifications related to shared projects and application activity.
  • To provide customer support.
  • To detect, prevent, and address security or technical problems.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve application performance.
  • To understand how the application is used and improve its features.
  • To measure advertising and application installation performance where applicable.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

SharedCost may share information with third-party service providers when necessary to provide application functionality. These providers may process information on our behalf, including for authentication, cloud storage, synchronization, notifications, analytics, diagnostics, and application security.

When you intentionally share a project with another participant, information associated with that project may be made available to those participants. This may include project information, participant information, expenses, deposits, and related project data.

We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, security, or property of SharedCost, our users, or others.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete your SharedCost account directly from within the application through the account or settings section.

When you delete your account, the associated SharedCost project and its related project data are scheduled for permanent deletion. The project and associated data may remain in our cloud systems for up to 30 days while the deletion process is completed.

When you delete individual project data, such as an expense or deposit, the deleted information is immediately removed from the application and is no longer displayed or available to you. The underlying data may remain in our cloud systems for up to 30 days before it is permanently deleted.

Analytics and diagnostic information may be retained separately by our service providers according to their applicable retention settings and policies.

Deleting the SharedCost application from your device does not itself delete your SharedCost account or cloud-stored information.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information without appropriate consent, please contact us.
